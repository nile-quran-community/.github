# 🌿 Nile Maqra'a

> **Good companionship, around the Quran, toward lasting impact.**

**Nile Maqra'a** is a community of university students brought together by a shared desire to strengthen their relationship with the Quran and live by its guidance.

We seek to build a lasting **righteous companionship** centered around the Quran, through memorization, recitation, tadabbur, weekly reflections, and mutual encouragement.

Our community exists to help young Muslims remain grounded in their faith, preserve their Islamic identity, grow in knowledge and character, and contribute positively to the Muslim Ummah.

We believe that the journey toward Allah is easier when we walk it together.

**Nile Maqra'a — gathered around the Quran.**
