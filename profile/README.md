<h1 align="center">Nile Maqra'a</h1>

<p align="center">   <strong>Good companionship, around the Quran, toward lasting impact.</strong> </p>

Nile Maqra'a is a community of university students gathered around the Quran.

We come together to memorize, recite, reflect, learn, and encourage one another to remain steadfast. We aim to keep the Quran at the center of our lives while growing in faith, character, and our contribution to the Muslim Ummah.

We believe that the journey toward Allah is better when we walk it together.

## 👋 Getting Involved

Maqra'a is built by its community. Whether through participating in Quran circles, attending reflections, helping organize activities, contributing to our media and research efforts, or building the technology that supports the community, there is a place for everyone who wants to contribute.

## 🤝 Our Community

We aim to create an environment where young Muslims can find good companionship, strengthen their relationship with the Quran, and grow alongside others who share the same pursuit.

Be sincere. Be useful. Encourage one another.

**And walk the path together.**
